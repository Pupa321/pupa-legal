Privacy Policy

Pupa is a baby month-age photo framing app. This Privacy Policy explains how Pupa handles information when you create keepsake frames on your device.

What Pupa is for

Pupa helps parents and caregivers take or import baby photos, apply month-age frames, stickers, captions, and collage layouts, then render and save a keepsake image. The product is designed to keep your creative work on the device you use.

Information stored on your device

Pupa may store the following locally in the app container:

  • Draft and finished frame projects, including captions, selected frame styles, and collage layouts
  • Photo copies you create inside Pupa for framing and rendering
  • Frame Credit balance and verified Apple purchase transaction identifiers used to prevent duplicate credit grants
  • Lightweight preferences such as whether you completed the welcome screen

Pupa does not require an account and does not operate a Pupa server that receives your baby photos.

Camera, Photos, Microphone, and Speech

These permissions are requested only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera — when you tap Take Photo to capture a baby photo for a frame
  • Photo Library — when you import a photo or save a finished frame to Photos
  • Microphone — when you use voice caption input
  • Speech Recognition — when spoken captions are converted to text

If you deny a permission, that feature is unavailable, but browsing existing frames and other unrelated features can still work.

Notifications

Pupa may show the system notification permission prompt. Pupa does not set a notification center delegate for push handling in this surface, and it does not use notification permission to send marketing or reminder campaigns from the A-side experience described here.

Purchases

Optional Frame Gifts, packs, chests, and bundles are sold as Apple consumable in-app purchases. Payment details are processed by Apple. Pupa stores only what is needed on device to grant credits after a verified transaction and to avoid granting the same transaction twice. There is no restore-purchases flow for consumable credits.

Analytics and tracking

Pupa does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use advertising tracking identifiers in this app surface.

Children and family photos

Pupa is intended for adults creating keepsakes of children in their care. You are responsible for the photos and captions you add. Do not upload or save content you are not allowed to use.

Data clearing

Clear All Data removes local projects, media copies stored by Pupa, purchase history identifiers kept by the app, preferences, and remaining Frame Credits on that installation.

Contact

For privacy questions about Pupa, contact the developer through the App Store product page support channel.
